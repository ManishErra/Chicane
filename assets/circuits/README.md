# Circuit silhouette asset credits

These files are simplified, monochrome derivatives of venue-specific circuit maps for CHICANE's small event-card overlays. Labels, background details, and non-track markings were removed; stroke styling was adjusted; path coordinates were rounded for compact display. The underlying circuit path geometry comes from the cited source maps.

## COTA — `cota.svg`

- Source: [F1 circuits 2014–2018 — Circuit of the Americas (version 2)](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:F1_circuits_2014-2018_-_Circuit_of_the_Americas_(version_2).svg)
- Author: Firkin, Openclipart
- License: [CC0 1.0](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/) — public-domain dedication. Credit is included here as a courtesy.
